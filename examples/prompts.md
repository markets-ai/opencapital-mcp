# Example prompts

Once OpenCapital is connected, ask in plain language — your assistant selects the right tool. These work across Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex, and any other MCP client.

## Segments & unit economics

- "What's Uber's Mobility take rate over the last 8 quarters?"
- "How does Airbnb's ADR compare across regions?"
- "Show me DoorDash Marketplace GOV every quarter since IPO."
- "Break down Amazon's revenue by segment for the last 3 years."

## Peer & cross-company comparison

- "Compare cloud revenue across AWS, Azure, GCP, and Oracle."
- "Compare gross margin across the Magnificent Seven for the last 8 quarters."
- "Which semiconductor companies grew revenue fastest last quarter?"

## Screening

- "Find me 10 large-caps growing revenue over 20% with positive free cash flow and a P/E under 30."
- "List profitable software companies with gross margins above 75%."
- "Which S&P sectors have the most companies trading below a P/E of 15?"

## Business understanding

- "What does Palantir actually do, and who are its main competitors?"
- "Before Nvidia reports, summarize what their suppliers, hyperscaler customers, and competitors said in their last filings."
- "Who competes with Datadog, and how do their filings describe each other?"

## Filing narrative search

- "What did Meta's last 10-K say about regulatory risk in the EU?"
- "Find every mention of 'backlog' in Datadog's recent filings."
- "Summarize the risk factors Boeing added in its most recent 10-K."

## Single-company deep dives

- "Show me Tesla's free cash flow, every quarter, for the last 5 years."
- "What does 'days sales outstanding' mean for Microsoft, and how has it trended?"
- "Pull Apple's gross margin, operating margin, and net margin side by side."

## Personal (requires sign-in)

- "Add NVDA, AMD, and AVGO to my watchlist."
- "What's on my watchlist, and which of them report earnings next?"
- "Look at my brokerage holdings and tell me my sector concentration."
